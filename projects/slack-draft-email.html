Slack Issue → Email Draft

An AI-assisted workflow that reviews a Slack thread, identifies the customer issue and its resolution, records the result and prepares a follow-up email draft.

SlackAIAirtablen8nWebhooks

Problem

Customer issues discussed in Slack can be difficult to track and follow up consistently, especially when the relevant context is spread across a thread.

Solution

The workflow receives a Slack event, retrieves and formats its thread, then uses an AI step to identify the issue and resolution. If the issue is unresolved, it posts an update to Slack. When it is resolved, the workflow finds the related order, records the customer issue in Airtable and creates a follow-up email draft.

Implementation highlights

  • Webhook-based Slack intake
  • Slack thread retrieval and formatting
  • AI-assisted issue and resolution extraction
  • Conditional handling for unresolved issues
  • Airtable order lookup and customer-issue update
  • Automated follow-up email draft

Why this matters

The workflow turns unstructured internal conversation into a traceable customer-service process, while keeping people in the loop when a resolution still needs attention.
