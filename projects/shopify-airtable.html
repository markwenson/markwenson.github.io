Shopify → Airtable Order Processing

An event-driven workflow that turns a Shopify order into structured customer, order and product records in Airtable, with operational Slack notifications.

n8nShopifyAirtableGoogle DriveJavaScriptWebhooksSlack

Problem

Order data can become fragmented when customer, order and product information has to be manually copied between systems. The workflow centralizes that handoff and applies different logic depending on whether a customer already exists.

Solution

The workflow receives a Shopify order, transforms the payload, searches for the customer in Airtable, branches for existing versus new customers, processes order details, and iterates through products before updating Airtable. After the Airtable loop completes, it creates the required Google Drive folders and notifies the team.
